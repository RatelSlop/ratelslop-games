The supplied GLB is bundled unchanged at models/backrooms.glb and includes
the original wallpaper, carpet and ceiling textures. The source description
credits the Backrooms texture style to Kane Pixels.

Adaptations in De Achterkamers:
- The source is treated as a modular kit: individual wall/baseboard, pillar,
  floor, ceiling and light meshes are extracted with their materials and UVs.
- Modules are normalized to a 3.15 metre ceiling and rearranged into a
  105.6 by 105.6 metre maze with nine randomized rooms and branching passages.
- Each new match rearranges walls and pillars into a fresh connected layout.
- Both ceiling tile and raised grid meshes retain their original geometry,
  materials and UVs; fluorescent emission follows timed power failures.
- Instancing reuses wall, ceiling, carpet and light geometry efficiently.
- Original materials use the game's lighting, fog and roughness adjustments.
- Collision is derived per wall/pillar instance, with connected navigation
  and reachable access passes. The original demo hallway and vent are omitted.
- Added game exit, access passes and photographic chasers.
