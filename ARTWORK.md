# Cover artwork and studio identity

The four game covers in `dist/art/covers/` were generated separately with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 2026-10-29. They are promotional illustrations, not game screenshots. Exact prompts are recorded in `dist/art/covers/provenance.json` and embedded in the WebP EXIF image descriptions. Original PNGs and prompts are provided separately in the task's outputs/game-covers directory.

- BouncyKoen: a voxel Dutch canal crossing with Koen, tulips, a yellow train and a seagull.
- ConciergeDienst: a school corridor at midnight, security camera and distant figure.
- UAVPilot: a fixed-wing drone above a countryside town at sunset.
- SchoolControl: the school adventure, with the pencil, chopsticks, dog and chicken companions. The cover does not enable publication of the private game source.

Official logo: https://github.com/RatelSlop/ratelslop.studio/blob/main/logo.png

Official favicon/mark: https://github.com/RatelSlop/ratelslop.studio/blob/main/favicon.svg

The original logo artwork is preserved unchanged (PNG source metadata is embedded), with files copied into `dist/brand/`. The logo is used in the header, studio section, footer, cover pages and thumbnails.
